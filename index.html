Saving

            
Pieces are saved in this browser, on this computer, automatically. Nothing is uploaded and there is
              no account. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes them — use Download project for a backup
              you keep yourself.

            
Two different “saves”

            
Download project = the whole piece, so you can carry on later or on another machine.
              Export = one recorded take: the audio, the typed text, and every key and control on one
              timeline.

            
What is guaranteed

            
The same performance log always produces the same notes. The ambience around them can vary a
              little between replays, and the audio is never sample-identical. Nothing here is random.
